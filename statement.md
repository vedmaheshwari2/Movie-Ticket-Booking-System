# Project Statement

## Project Title
Movie Ticket Booking System

## Problem Statement
Customers need a simple way to select a movie, book tickets, choose seats, receive applicable offers, and maintain reward credits. A manual process can lead to duplicate seat allocation, incorrect calculations, and difficulty maintaining customer credit information.

## Scope
The project covers movie selection, ticket booking, seat allocation, discount calculation, free-ticket offers, customer movie credits, persistent booking records, validation, and receipt generation.

## Target Users
- Movie customers
- Small cinema operators
- Students demonstrating Python programming concepts

## High-Level Features
1. Customer and movie-credit management
2. Movie and ticket selection
3. Seat availability and validation
4. Discount and free-ticket processing
5. Booking receipt generation
6. JSON-based persistent storage
7. Automated validation tests
